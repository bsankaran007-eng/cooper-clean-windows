## Brand & Style

The brand personality is dependable, pristine, energetic, and community-rooted. It serves homeowners and commercial property managers throughout Tyler and Lindale, Texas, who value pristine curb appeal, reliable service, and straightforward local craftsmanship. The interface balances high-performance utility with reassuring professionalism.

The design style combines **Modern Crisp Utility** with **Subtle Glassmorphic Fluidity**. It evokes the refreshing clarity of clean water and gleaming glass:
- Ultra-clean surfaces featuring pure whites and luminous sky-blue accents.
- Structured deep navy foundations grounding service tiers, pricing, and trust badges.
- High-visibility action triggers engineered specifically for high-conversion field inquiries and instant mobile estimates.
- Friendly, polished micro-details such as pill badges, rounded cards, and tactile CTA surfaces that signal modern credibility without sacrificing warmth.

## Colors

The palette directly communicates water, pressure washing, and architectural clarity:

- **Primary (`#0284c7`)**: Water Jet Blue. Deployed for primary conversion actions, active tabs, interactive highlights, and verification accents.
- **Secondary (`#0f172a`)**: Deep Navy Foundation. Provides authoritative contrast for hero headers, footer canvases, high-tier pricing cards, and dominant typography.
- **Tertiary (`#38bdf8`)**: Sky Sheen Highlight. Used for vibrant badges, subtle gradient overlays, focus ring glows, and energetic status badges.
- **Neutral Canvas (`#f8fafc`)**: Crisp Cool Slates. Creates clean breathing room across backgrounds, card surfaces, and alternating list sections (`#ffffff` for elevated cards, `#f1f5f9` for subtle structural dividers, and `#64748b` for secondary descriptive copy).
- **Energetic Alert/Highlight (`#f59e0b` / `#10b981`)**: Sunbeam Amber for rating stars and limited-time seasonal slots; Pine Green for instant confirmation badges and verified guarantee tags.

## Typography

Plus Jakarta Sans is utilized across display, body, and UI labels. Its geometric precision and contemporary rounded terminals deliver high legibility and approachable, friendly authority.

- **Headlines**: Set in bold to extrabold weights with tight tracking (`-0.02em`) to command focus on mobile viewports.
- **Body Content**: Employs comfortable line heights (1.5–1.55x) with neutral slate tints (`#334155`) against white backgrounds for effortless outdoor sunlight readability.
- **Service & Trust Badges**: Use uppercase or title case with slightly widened tracking (`+0.03em`) on `label-sm` to maintain high scannability within compact mobile rows.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout operates on an 8-point harmonic rhythm engineered for a **mobile-first conversion pipeline**.

- **Mobile (< 768px)**: 4-column fluid layout with a base canvas margin of `1rem` (`16px`) and `1rem` gutters. Touch targets adhere strictly to a minimum 48px standard.
- **Tablet (768px – 1023px)**: 8-column fluid layout with `2rem` outer margins and `1.5rem` gutters. Form fields switch to dual-column side-by-side configurations.
- **Desktop (1024px+)**: 12-column grid maxing out at `1200px` content container, with `3rem` outer margins. Content groups (e.g., Before/After galleries, service breakdowns) reflow naturally into 3-column and 4-column card matrices.
- **Fixed Persistent Bar**: Mobile devices maintain a sticky bottom quick-contact action panel using `space-sm` vertical padding with immediate one-tap calling and instant estimate booking.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses **luminous tonal layers and ambient water-tinted drop shadows** to reproduce the pristine clarity of spotless glass surfaces:

- **Level 0 (Flat Canvas)**: Neutral surface `#f8fafc` directly on background without shadow.
- **Level 1 (Crisp Card)**: `#ffffff` surface with a delicate border (`1px solid #e2e8f0`) and subtle ambient shadow: `0 2px 8px -2px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04), 0 1px 4px -1px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.02)`.
- **Level 2 (Hover & Interactive Containers)**: Raised state for quote calculators, service cards, and modal dialogs: `0 12px 24px -6px rgba(2, 132, 199, 0.08), 0 4px 12px -2px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.05)`.
- **Level 3 (Sticky CTA Bar & Lead Overlays)**: High-prominence mobile elements with sky-blue edge glows: `0 20px 32px -8px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.12), 0 0 0 1px rgba(56, 189, 248, 0.15)`.

## Shapes

The design uses an approachable **Rounded Corner Language** (Level 2):

- **Standard Elements (0.5rem / 8px)**: Applied to input controls, standard service list cards, service item containers, and accordion panels.
- **Card Containers (`rounded-lg` - 1rem / 16px)**: Applied to featured packages, testimonial carousels, and quote generation containers.
- **Badges, Pills & Primary CTAs (`rounded-full`)**: Micro-badges (e.g., "Tyler & Lindale Local", "Free Instant Estimate"), phone action pills, and floating action triggers utilize continuous pill shapes for a welcoming, frictionless touch response.

## Components

### Buttons & Action Triggers
- **Primary CTA**: `#0284c7` solid background, white text (`label-lg`), pill-rounded or `0.5rem` radius, minimum 48px height. On hover: shifts to `#0369a1` with an elevated blue ambient halo (`0 6px 16px -2px rgba(2, 132, 199, 0.4)`).
- **Secondary CTA**: Deep Navy (`#0f172a`) solid with crisp white text for high-contrast phone calls or alternative scheduling options.
- **Ghost/Outline**: Transparent background, `1.5px solid #0284c7`, text color `#0284c7`, with light sky background tint on press (`rgba(56, 189, 248, 0.08)`).

### Service Cards & Packages
- Built on `#ffffff` backdrops with a crisp `1px solid #e2e8f0` border and `rounded-lg` (16px) corners.
- Features a localized service badge at the top-left (e.g., "Pressure Washing", "Window Glaze Treatment"), prominent service hero icon in light blue circular wrapper (`#e0f2fe`), concise checklist items, and an immediate bottom CTA button.

### Badges & Trust Seals
- Compact pill-shaped pills featuring `label-sm`.
- **Verified Local Tag**: Pale sky background (`#e0f2fe`), dark blue text (`#0369a1`), paired with an SVG checkmark icon.
- **Rating Stars Badge**: Pale gold background (`#fef3c7`), dark amber text (`#92400e`), displaying 5-star ratings and Google review tallies.

### Input Fields & Estimate Sliders
- White background with `1.5px solid #cbd5e1` resting state and `rounded-md` (8px) corners.
- Active focus state: border shifts to `#0284c7` with a non-intrusive 3px sky outline (`rgba(56, 189, 248, 0.25)`).
- Mobile numeric controls (square footage / story count) feature oversized tap buttons (+/-) for hassle-free thumb interaction.

### Before & After Interactive Comparison Slider
- Distinctive component for exterior cleaning: a split-image comparison slider with a pill-shaped divider handle colored in Sky Blue (`#38bdf8`), encased with drop shadow and drag arrows to immediately showcase washing efficacy.

### Sticky Mobile Action Bar
- Docked directly at the screen base. Houses dual high-urgency touch actions: "Call Now" in `#0f172a` with a tap-to-call direct link, alongside "Instant Quote" in vibrant `#0284c7`.
